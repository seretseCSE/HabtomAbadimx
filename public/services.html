What We Offer

Our Global Services

End-to-end trade solutions — from sourcing to delivery, we handle every step of the journey.

Agricultural Commodity Export

We source, grade, and export premium Ethiopian agricultural commodities including coffee, sesame, grains, and pulses to buyers worldwide.

  • ✓ Direct farm sourcing
  • ✓ Quality grading & sorting
  • ✓ Phytosanitary certification
🚜

Agricultural Equipment Import

We import and supply tractors, irrigation systems, harvesters, and other farming machinery tailored for Ethiopian agricultural conditions.

  • ✓ Sourced from trusted manufacturers
  • ✓ Customs clearance support
  • ✓ After-sales & spare parts

Freight & Logistics

We manage the full logistics chain — from inland transport in Ethiopia to sea freight and final delivery to international ports.

  • ✓ FCL & LCL sea freight
  • ✓ Port of Djibouti handling
  • ✓ Real-time shipment tracking
📋

Customs & Documentation

We handle all customs procedures, export permits, and documentation so you can focus on your business while we handle the paperwork.

  • ✓ Export permits & licenses
  • ✓ Bill of lading, COO, COA
  • ✓ Customs clearance

Quality Inspection & Testing

Every shipment is tested and graded by certified inspectors before loading. We ensure your cargo meets international food safety and quality standards.

  • ✓ SGS / ETQAE inspection
  • ✓ Moisture & purity testing
  • ✓ Certificate of Analysis

Trade Consultancy

New to trading with Ethiopia? We guide international buyers and local suppliers through the entire process — regulations, pricing, and partnerships.

  • ✓ Market entry guidance
  • ✓ Supplier matching
  • ✓ Contract negotiation support
